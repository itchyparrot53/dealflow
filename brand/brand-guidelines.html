Dealflow brand

The leaf mark: a rounded leaf with one sharp corner, carrying an arrow pointing up and to the right. Growth, forward motion, deals moving through the pipeline.

Logo

Horizontal, full colour (default)
Reverse, for dark backgrounds
Stacked, for square spaces
White, on brand green or photos
Black, single-colour print
Mark and app icon

Colour

Green#2D8548 · RGB 45 133 72
Light green#58CC85 · RGB 88 204 133
Indigo#211C52 · RGB 33 28 82
White#FFFFFF

Green with white text passes WCAG AA (4.6:1) at 14px bold and above. Use light green only on indigo or other dark backgrounds.

Typeface

Inter, weight 600 for the wordmark (outlined in the logo files, so no font is needed to display them). Inter 400 to 600 for product and marketing text. Licence: SIL Open Font License 1.1.

Clear space and minimum size

Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the arrow (a quarter of the leaf height) on every side. Minimum sizes: horizontal logo 96px wide on screen (25mm in print); mark alone 16px (favicon artwork is tuned for it).

Don't

  • Recolour the leaf outside the brand palette, or put the green leaf on indigo (use the reverse version).
  • Stretch, rotate, outline, add shadows or effects.
  • Rearrange the mark and wordmark, change the gap, or retype "Dealflow" in another font.
  • Place the full-colour logo on busy photos (use the white version on a darkened area).
  • Change the arrow direction or round the leaf's sharp corner.
