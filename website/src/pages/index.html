How it works

Every step of the deal, covered

See your whole pipeline at once

Each stage is a column and each deal a card, with the value, the contact and a dot that tells you whether the next step is planned.

  • Drag to move a deal, with undo if you slip
  • Weighted totals per stage and a forecast by month
  • Rotting deals highlighted before they go cold
More about the pipeline

Everything about a deal on one screen

Activities, notes, email, files, products, quotes and the full history sit together, with an AI summary of risks and next steps at the top.

  • Generate a summary and turn next steps into activities
  • Log calls with their outcome in two clicks
  • Mark won or lost with a reason, and reopen any time
More about AI

Your inbox, linked to your deals

Connect any IMAP and SMTP mailbox with an app password. Replies stay threaded and go out through your own mail server.

  • Templates, signatures and send later
  • Open and link tracking with notifications
  • Unmatched email surfaced so nothing slips
More about email

From quote to paid invoice

Turn a deal's products into a numbered quote. Your customer accepts it online by typing their name, and the invoice follows in one click.

  • Multi-currency with your own exchange rates
  • Viewed, accepted and paid notifications
  • Overdue invoices flagged automatically
