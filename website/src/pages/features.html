Features

Everything your team needs to win deals

One workspace for the whole sales cycle, from first enquiry to paid invoice. Every feature below works today and is covered in the user guide.

{{icon:kanban}}

Pipeline and deals

A board with one column per stage, totals and weighted values, and a clear next step on every card.

  • Several pipelines with your own stages, probabilities and rotting limits
  • Board, list and archived views with filters, saved filters and CSV export
  • Deal workspace with activities, notes, email, files, products and history
  • Leads inbox with one-click conversion to a deal
  • Forecast by expected close month, with weighted and won totals
Pipelines and deals guide
{{icon:calendar}}

Activities and calendar

Calls, meetings, tasks, deadlines, emails and lunches, scheduled against the right deal or contact.

  • Overdue, Today, This week and To-do views
  • Day, week and month calendar
  • Repeating activities that create the next one when you finish
  • Call logging with outcome, direction and duration
Activities guide
{{icon:users}}

Contacts and organizations

People and companies with their deals, activity and email in one place.

  • Saved views, filters and plain-English filters
  • Contacts timeline that shows who has gone quiet
  • Duplicate finder with merge
  • CSV import with undo, and CSV export
Contacts guide
{{icon:mail}}

Email

Two-way email over IMAP and SMTP with any provider that supports app passwords.

  • Inbox, drafts, outbox, sent and archive, synced from your mailbox
  • Conversations linked to deals and contacts automatically
  • Templates, signatures, merge fields and send later
  • Open and link tracking, follow-up labels and private threads
Email guide
{{icon:doc}}

Quotes, invoices and products

Documents built from a deal's products, numbered, sent and tracked to payment.

  • Product catalogue with codes, prices, currencies and tax
  • Public quote page where customers accept or decline
  • Invoices with manual payments, overdue status and void
  • Print preview and PDF download
Quotes and invoices guide
{{icon:chart}}

Reports, goals and forecast

From a quick performance check to dashboards you build yourself.

  • Performance page: deals won, win rate, average deal, sales cycle
  • Goals for the company, a team or a person
  • Insights dashboards and reports with charts, tables and CSV export
  • AI explanations of what a report shows
Reporting guide
{{icon:sparkle}}

AI features

AI built into the places you already work, through OpenRouter, with limits you control.

  • Assistant chat that answers from your data and asks before changing it
  • Deal summaries with risks and next steps, and deal scoring
  • Email drafts, notes turned into activities, filters from plain English
  • Monthly token limits per company and per user, with usage reports
AI guide
{{icon:bolt}}

Automations and webhooks

Routine work done for you, and events sent wherever you need them.

  • Triggers, conditions and up to ten actions, including AI steps
  • Start from a template or describe the automation in a sentence
  • Run log and protection against automation loops
  • Signed webhooks with retries and a delivery log
Automations guide
{{icon:form}}

Web forms and booking pages

Public pages that bring new business straight into your pipeline.

  • Forms that create a lead or deal, embeddable, with spam protection
  • Booking pages with weekly availability, buffers and time zones
  • Bookings land in your calendar, with a cancel link for the guest
Forms and booking guide
{{icon:folder}}

Projects

Deliver what you sold. Start a project from a won deal and track it phase by phase.

  • Phases and tasks with progress
  • Linked to the deal, person and organization
  • Owners, start and due dates, and status
Projects guide
{{icon:shield}}

Admin, security and data

The controls an admin needs to run a CRM with confidence.

  • Users, teams, permission sets and visibility groups
  • Custom fields, labels, lost reasons and activity types
  • Audit log, recycle bin with 30-day restore, and daily backups
  • REST API with personal tokens, and MCP access for AI tools
Admin guide
{{icon:phone}}

Mobile and offline

A phone-friendly layout you can install like an app.

  • Bottom navigation for deals, activities, inbox and contacts
  • Installable from the browser on phones and desktops
  • Recently opened records stay readable offline
Mobile guide
