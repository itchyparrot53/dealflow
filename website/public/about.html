About

A CRM for teams who want to own their pipeline

Dealflow is a business development CRM for one company per install. It gives sales and business development teams a fast, focused place to manage deals, contacts and email, with AI built in and nothing hidden behind a plan.

Self-hosted

Dealflow runs on a server you control with Docker Compose. Your customer data never has to leave it.

Single company

One install serves one company. Invite as many people as you like, with permissions and visibility to match your team.

AI included

Summaries, scores, drafts and an assistant, through the AI provider you choose, with monthly limits you set.

Privacy

How Dealflow treats your data

Usage telemetry and update checks are off by default. Data leaves your server only for the services you configure: your mail server, your AI provider for AI features, and any contact enrichment provider an admin chooses to enable.

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does Dealflow cost?

Nothing. Dealflow is free and released under the MIT licence. You pay only for the server you run it on and any services you connect, such as your AI provider.

Is there a hosted version?

No. Dealflow is self-hosted by design, so each company runs its own install and keeps its own data.

Which email providers work?

Any provider that offers IMAP and SMTP with an app password. Dealflow has a ready-made setting for Purelymail, and an "Other IMAP/SMTP server" option for everything else.

Which AI does it use?

Dealflow connects to AI models through OpenRouter with your own API key, so you choose the model. Admins can turn AI off or set monthly limits for the company and for each user.

Will the AI change my data?

Only when you confirm. The assistant shows each change with Confirm and Reject buttons, and it only sees the records you can see.

Can I bring my existing data?

Yes. Import people, organizations, deals, leads and activities from CSV files, map the columns, preview and import. A bad import can be undone in one click.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. Dealflow has a phone layout and can be installed from the browser like an app. Records you opened recently stay readable when you are offline.

How do I get help?

Start with the user guide, which covers every feature step by step, or the troubleshooting page.
