Getting started

This section covers signing in for the first time, finding your way around Dealflow, and the shortcuts that save time every day.

PDF

Pipelines and deals

A pipeline is the path your deals follow, split into stages. The board shows one column per stage, with the total value, number of deals and weighted value at the top of each column.

PDF

Activities and calendar

Activities are the calls, meetings, tasks, deadlines, emails and lunches that move deals forward. Every activity has a subject, a type and a due date, and can be linked to a deal, person or organization.

PDF

Contacts and organizations

People are the individuals you deal with. Organizations are the companies they work for. Both live under Contacts in the left rail.

PDF

Email

Dealflow connects to your own mailbox over IMAP and SMTP, so email you send and receive appears in Dealflow, threaded and linked to the right deals and contacts. Replies from Dealflow go out through your normal mail server and land in your Sent folder.

PDF

Notifications

Dealflow tells you when something needs your attention: you were mentioned, an activity was assigned to you, a deal you follow was won, a customer viewed or accepted a quote, an email you sent was opened, and more.

PDF

Quotes and invoices

Dealflow builds quotes and invoices from a deal's products, sends them to your customer by email with a link to a public page, and tracks them from draft to paid. You need the documents permission to create and send them.

PDF

Projects

Projects track the work that follows a won deal, such as onboarding or delivery. A project has phases (for example Kickoff, Delivery and Wrap-up), and each phase holds tasks.

PDF

Forms and booking pages

Web forms and booking pages are public pages that people outside your company can use without signing in.

PDF

AI features

Dealflow has AI built in. It can summarise a deal, score how likely it is to close, draft emails, turn meeting notes into activities, build filters from plain English, explain reports and answer questions in a chat. An admin can turn AI off or set monthly limits (see Admin).

PDF

Admin

This section is for admins: the people who set Dealflow up for the company. Most of these pages are under Settings (open the Account menu and choose Settings). The settings menu has two parts: My account (your own preferences, which every user has) and Company overview (company-wide settings, shown only to users with the right permissions).

PDF

Self-hosting and install

This page is for the person who runs the Dealflow server. Dealflow is self-hosted and single-company: one install serves one company, and your data stays on your own machine.

Glossary

Term Meaning
Activity Something to do or something that happened: a call, meeting, task, deadline, email or lunch. Activities have a due date and can be marked done.
Admin A user who can change company-wide settings. Admins have every permission.
Assistant The AI chat panel. It can look things up in Dealflow and, after you confirm, make changes for you.
Base currency The currency Dealflow converts every total into (for example GBP). Other currencies use the manual exchange rates an admin sets.
Board The pipeline view with one column per stage and one card per deal.
Booking page A public page where someone outside your company picks a time to meet you.
Deal A potential sale you are working on. Every deal sits in one stage of one pipeline and has a value, an owner and usually a person and organization.
Follower A user who gets notifications about a record without owning it.
Forecast The view that groups open deals by the month they are expected to close.
Insights Dealflow's reporting area: dashboards made of charts, and reports you build yourself.
Label A coloured tag you add to deals, leads or contacts to group them.
Lead An enquiry that is not yet a qualified deal. Leads live in the Leads list until you convert them to a deal or archive them.
Lost reason Why a deal was lost. Admins keep the list of reasons.
Organization A company you do business with. People can belong to an organization.
Owner The user responsible for a record.
Permission set A named group of permissions (for example Regular or Admin) given to users.
Person A contact: someone you deal with.
Pipeline A sequence of stages that deals move through, from first contact to won or lost. A company can have several pipelines.
Probability How likely a deal in a stage is to close, as a percentage. It is used for weighted totals.
Quote / Invoice Documents you build from a deal's products and send to a customer.
Rotting A deal that has stayed in a stage longer than the stage allows. Rotting deals are highlighted on the board.
Stage One step in a pipeline, such as Qualified or Proposal Made.
Visibility group A group that controls which records its members can see.
Weighted value A deal's value multiplied by its stage probability.
Won / Lost The two ways a deal closes. Won and lost deals leave the board but can be reopened.
